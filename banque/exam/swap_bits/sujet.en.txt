Assignment name    : swap_bits
Expected files     : swap_bits.c
Allowed functions  : none
--------------------------------------------------------------

Write a function that swaps the two halves of octet: the 4 high bits take
the place of the 4 low bits, and the other way round.

For example, 01000001 becomes 00010100.

Your function must have this prototype:

unsigned char	swap_bits(unsigned char octet);

The tester calls it from its own main: do not submit one.
