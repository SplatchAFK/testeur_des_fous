Assignment name    : reverse_bits
Expected files     : reverse_bits.c
Allowed functions  : none
--------------------------------------------------------------

Write a function that returns octet with its 8 bits in reverse order: the
highest bit becomes the lowest, and so on.

For example, 00100110 becomes 01100100.

Your function must have this prototype:

unsigned char	reverse_bits(unsigned char octet);

The tester calls it from its own main: do not submit one.
