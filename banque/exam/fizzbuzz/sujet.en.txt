Assignment name    : fizzbuzz
Expected files     : fizzbuzz.c
Allowed functions  : write
--------------------------------------------------------------

Write a program that displays the numbers from 1 to 100, one per line.

Three rules replace some of the numbers:
- a multiple of 3 is replaced by "fizz";
- a multiple of 5 is replaced by "buzz";
- a multiple of both 3 and 5 is replaced by "fizzbuzz".

The program ignores its arguments.

Start of the expected output:

$> ./fizzbuzz
1
2
fizz
4
buzz
fizz
7
[...]
14
fizzbuzz
16
[...]
