Assignment name    : ft_strcasecmp
Expected files     : ft_strcasecmp.c
Allowed functions  : malloc, free, write, ta libft
--------------------------------------------------------------

Compare s1 and s2 ignoring case, same sign as strcasecmp(3).

Your function must have this prototype:

int		ft_strcasecmp(const char *s1, const char *s2);

Notion : unsigned char et tolower
Norme obligatoire. Les fonctions d'aide doivent être static.
