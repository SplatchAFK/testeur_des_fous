Assignment name    : ft_strspn
Expected files     : ft_strspn.c
Allowed functions  : malloc, free, write, your libft
--------------------------------------------------------------

Length of the initial segment of s made only of characters from accept, like strspn(3).

Your function must have this prototype:

size_t	ft_strspn(const char *s, const char *accept);

Concept: nested search, like the libc
The Norm is mandatory. Helper functions must be static.
