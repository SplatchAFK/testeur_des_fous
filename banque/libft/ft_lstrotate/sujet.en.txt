Assignment name    : ft_lstrotate
Expected files     : ft_lstrotate.c
Allowed functions  : malloc, free, write, ta libft
--------------------------------------------------------------

Rotate the list by n (negative goes backwards, n may exceed the size or be INT_MIN).

Your function must have this prototype:

void	ft_lstrotate(t_list **lst, int n);

Notion : modulo, négatifs, relier la queue
Norme obligatoire. Les fonctions d'aide doivent être static.
